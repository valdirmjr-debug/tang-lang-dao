## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS on Netlify (SiteCaio5 version, November 2026, from the owner's spec "Ajustes para o novo modelo de site"). One Netlify Function (`netlify/functions/visitas.mjs`, Netlify Blobs) counts visits for the footer. Pages are generated from sources by a build script; data files `assets/dados-alunos.js`, `dados-hall.js` and `dados-grade.js` come from the owner's spreadsheets and curriculum PDF. Do not deploy or change the domain without the owner asking.

## Users

Primary: people in the north zone of São Paulo (Taipas, Jardim Alvina, Pirituba and around) who are thinking of starting Kung Fu, mostly with no experience, on a phone. They need to feel welcome, understand what this is, and message on WhatsApp.
Secondary: practitioners and other schools interested in the Louva-Deus style and its genealogy.

## Product Purpose

Instituto de Artes Marciais Tang Lang Dao (螳螂道武术协会) teaches and promotes Chinese martial arts and culture, centred on Louva-Deus (Táng Láng Quán 螳螂拳). The site exists to bring in new students.

Success: more WhatsApp messages and visits to Taipas.

## Positioning

An innovative teaching system: the curriculum is split into mandatory and elective content, so the student chooses their own martial path (fight, philosophy, or health). Founded in 2024 by Laoshi Caio Petrelis and Laoshi Vinicius David, combining a technical structure with the scientific principles of Physical Education. The lineage is documented back to Wang Lang.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pages: Início, Sobre Nós, Locais de Treino, Modalidades (Kung Fu, Tai Chi), Genealogia, Portal do Aluno (Lista de Alunos, Hall da Fama, Acesso às Aulas), Corpo Docente. Menu groups open on hover or click. Acesso às Aulas is a browser-only prototype; see PORTAL.md for the real structure.
- Language: Brazilian Portuguese, with Chinese characters and pinyin kept as the institute writes them.
- The class schedule is published on Início and Locais de Treino; no prices are published. Details of the curriculum: "Consulte nossos representantes".

## Brand Commitments

- Owner decision: page backgrounds must be soft and easy on the eyes. The academy's orange #F77A01 and green #13632B stay in the logo and the mantis; surfaces use softened tints (see DESIGN.md). Lotus magenta #FE005C only for "As 5 Pétalas".
- Logo: mantis, pagoda and ring, must be preserved.
- User's bar: Awwwards level, people should be surprised.
- Voice: respectful, plain, welcoming; honest about what is legend and what is history.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real photos: both teachers, the Taipas façade, Master Luo Guang Yu (historic). Logo. Lotus diagram. On the old Google Sites.
- Genealogy transcribed from the owner's file "Árvore Genealógica.drawio.pdf" (last updated 02/11/2026): 75 names, from Wang Lang to Caio Petrelis. Colours mark 9 lineages; solid lines are "Legado direto", dashed lines are "Treinou o estilo". Update the page from this file, not from memory.
- Tables: 12 Princípios and curriculum techniques. Mission, vision, 5 Petals. Bibliography.
- Owner decision: remove AI illustrations and third-party images (cartoon panda, Bruce Lee, Kung Fu TV show). New art is authored in code.
- No testimonials, student counts, prices, competition results or teacher ranks. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. A beginner should feel this is for them, not only for fighters.
2. Show the lineage and the method; do not boast.
3. History stays honest: legend is called legend.
4. WhatsApp is always one tap away.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard operable, respects reduced motion, readable contrast, Chinese text marked with its language, static fallback for every effect.
